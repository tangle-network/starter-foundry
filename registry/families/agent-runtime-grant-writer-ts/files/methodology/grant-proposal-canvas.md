# Grant Proposal Canvas

## 1. Problem Statement
- What is the specific problem you are addressing?
- Who is affected? (geography, demographics, scale)
- Why is this problem urgent now?

## 2. Theory of Change
- If we do X, then Y will happen because Z.
- Map inputs → activities → outputs → outcomes → impact.

## 3. Activities
- What will you do with the grant funds?
- Timeline: key milestones.
- Who will execute? (staff, partners, volunteers)

## 4. Outcomes & Evaluation
- Primary outcome: measurable change.
- Secondary outcomes.
- How will you measure success? (indicators, data sources, frequency)

## 5. Budget Narrative
- Total request.
- Major line items (personnel, materials, travel, indirect).
- Justification: why each cost is necessary.

## 6. Organizational Capacity
- Brief history and mission.
- Relevant experience (past grants, programs).
- Key staff qualifications.

## 7. Sustainability
- How will the work continue after the grant?
- Other funding sources.

*Use this canvas to structure each proposal. Fill sections with the operator's input. Flag gaps for the operator to address.*
