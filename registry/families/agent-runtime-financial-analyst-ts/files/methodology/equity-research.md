# Equity Research Template

## Purpose
Produce a concise equity research report on a public company.

## Sections
1. **Business Overview** — what the company does, revenue model, competitive position.
2. **Industry & Market** — TAM, growth rate, key trends, regulatory environment.
3. **Financial Analysis** — revenue growth, margins, ROIC, leverage, cash flow.
4. **Valuation** — DCF, comps, precedent transactions. Show a range.
5. **Risks** — company-specific, industry, macro.
6. **Catalysts** — upcoming events that could move the stock.
7. **Thesis** — bull case, bear case, base case.

## Output
- A structured report with clear sections.
- All financial data sourced from public filings (10-K, 10-Q, earnings calls).
- Valuation range with key assumptions.

## Refusal
Do not write a report on a private company without public filings. Do not fabricate data.
