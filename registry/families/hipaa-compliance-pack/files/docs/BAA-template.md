# Business Associate Agreement — Template

> **This is a template, not legal advice.** Your legal team MUST review and
> customize before execution. This document exists so product teams have a
> starting point — do not ship it as-is.

## Parties

- **Covered Entity**: [Name, address, Tax ID]
- **Business Associate**: [Name, address, Tax ID]
- **Effective Date**: [Date]

## 1. Definitions

Terms used but not defined have the meaning assigned in 45 CFR Part 160
and Part 164 (the HIPAA Rules).

## 2. Permitted uses and disclosures

Business Associate may use or disclose PHI:

- To perform the services described in the underlying Services Agreement.
- For the proper management and administration of Business Associate's business.
- To provide data aggregation services relating to the health care operations of the Covered Entity.

All other uses and disclosures are prohibited.

## 3. Business Associate obligations

Business Associate agrees to:

- Not use or disclose PHI other than as permitted by this agreement or as required by law.
- Use appropriate safeguards, including technical safeguards under §164.312.
- Report to the Covered Entity any use or disclosure not provided for by this agreement within [5] business days of discovery.
- Ensure any subcontractors that create, receive, maintain, or transmit PHI on behalf of Business Associate agree to the same restrictions and conditions.
- Make PHI available to the Covered Entity as required for §164.524 (access), §164.526 (amendment), and §164.528 (accounting of disclosures).
- At termination, return or destroy all PHI received from the Covered Entity and retain no copies in any form.

## 4. Security incident notification

Business Associate shall notify the Covered Entity of any security incident
of which it becomes aware within [2] business days. Security incident has
the meaning set forth in §164.304.

## 5. Breach notification

Following the discovery of a breach of Unsecured PHI, Business Associate
shall notify the Covered Entity without unreasonable delay and in no case
later than 60 calendar days after discovery.

## 6. Term and termination

- **Term**: This agreement is effective as of the Effective Date and continues until all PHI is returned or destroyed.
- **Termination for cause**: The Covered Entity may terminate upon 30 days notice for material breach uncured within that period.
