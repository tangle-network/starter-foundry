# Competitive Technical Positioning Template

## Purpose
Position the product against competitors using honest technical comparisons.

## Sections

### 1. Competitor Overview
- Competitor name and product
- Their primary use case and target customer
- Their technical architecture (if known)

### 2. Comparison Matrix
| Dimension | Our Product | Competitor | Advantage | Evidence |
|-----------|-------------|------------|-----------|----------|
| Performance | <100ms p95 | <300ms p95 | 3x faster | Benchmarks from [source] |
| Scalability | 10k req/s | 5k req/s | 2x capacity | Load test results |
| Integration | REST + GraphQL | REST only | More flexible | API docs |
| Security | SOC2, HIPAA | SOC2 only | Broader compliance | Certifications |

### 3. Key Differentiators
- **Technical differentiator 1**: Explain why it matters to the prospect
- **Technical differentiator 2**: Explain why it matters to the prospect
- **Technical differentiator 3**: Explain why it matters to the prospect

### 4. Competitive Objections
- Objection 1: "Competitor claims X" → Our response with evidence
- Objection 2: "Competitor has feature Y" → Our response (acknowledge, then pivot to our strengths)

### 5. Positioning Narrative
- **Opening**: "When evaluating solutions, we know you're considering [competitor]. Here's how we compare on the dimensions that matter most to you..."
- **Body**: Walk through the comparison matrix, focusing on the prospect's top criteria
- **Close**: "Based on your requirements, we believe our approach gives you [specific benefit] that [competitor] cannot match."

### 6. Supporting Materials
- Benchmark reports
- Case studies
- Architecture whitepapers
- Third-party reviews
