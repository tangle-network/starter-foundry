# Incident Post-Mortem Template

## Principles
- Blameless: focus on systems, not people.
- Actionable: every finding leads to a concrete fix.
- Timely: write within 48 hours of resolution.

## Sections
1. **Summary** — What happened, impact (users affected, duration, severity).
2. **Timeline** — Key events: detection, response, mitigation, resolution.
3. **Root cause** — The systemic failure (not the person who pushed the button).
4. **Contributing factors** — Monitoring gaps, process gaps, knowledge gaps.
5. **Action items** — Each with owner, due date, and type (preventive, detective, process).
6. **Lessons learned** — What went well, what could be improved.

## Follow-up
- Track action items to completion in the team's backlog.
- Share post-mortem with relevant stakeholders (eng, ops, product).
