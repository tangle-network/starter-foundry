# 1:1 Cadence Template

## Purpose
Build trust, surface blockers, and align on growth. This is not a status update — it's a structured conversation.

## Frequency
Weekly, 30 minutes. No cancellations; reschedule if needed.

## Agenda (suggested)
1. **Check-in** (2 min) — How are you doing? Energy, focus, anything on your mind.
2. **Tactical** (10 min) — Blockers, dependencies, decisions needed from me.
3. **Growth** (10 min) — Skills they want to build, stretch assignments, feedback on recent work.
4. **Strategic** (5 min) — Team direction, org changes, long-term concerns.
5. **Close** (3 min) — Action items, next 1:1 date, anything they want to add.

## Notes
- Let the direct report set the agenda 24h before.
- Capture action items in a shared doc.
- Rotate between tactical and growth-heavy weeks.
