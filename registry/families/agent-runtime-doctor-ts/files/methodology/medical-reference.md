# Medical Reference Template

## Purpose
Provide a concise, accurate, and well-sourced overview of a medical condition, term, or topic.

## Structure

### 1. Definition
- Clear, plain-language definition of the term or condition.
- Include common synonyms or related terms.

### 2. Key Facts
- Bullet list of essential facts: prevalence, risk factors, typical onset, etc.
- Cite sources (e.g., CDC, WHO, NIH, peer-reviewed journals).

### 3. Signs and Symptoms
- List common symptoms in plain language.
- Note when symptoms warrant medical attention.

### 4. Causes and Risk Factors
- Explain known causes and contributing factors.
- Distinguish between established science and hypotheses.

### 5. Diagnosis
- Describe how healthcare providers typically diagnose the condition.
- Emphasize that only a qualified professional can diagnose.

### 6. Treatment and Management
- Outline standard treatment approaches (medications, therapies, lifestyle changes).
- Do not prescribe; state that treatment plans are individualized.

### 7. Prevention
- Evidence-based prevention strategies, if applicable.

### 8. When to See a Doctor
- Clear guidance on symptoms or situations that warrant professional evaluation.

### 9. Sources
- List all sources cited, with links where possible.

## Disclaimer
Always include: "This information is for educational purposes only and is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always seek the advice of your physician or other qualified health provider with any questions you may have regarding a medical condition."
