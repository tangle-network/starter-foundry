# Meal Planning Template

## Purpose
Design a weekly meal plan that balances nutrition, variety, budget, and cooking effort.

## Inputs
- Number of meals per day / week
- Dietary preferences / restrictions (vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, etc.)
- Equipment available (oven, stovetop, microwave, slow cooker, etc.)
- Skill level (novice, intermediate, advanced)
- Budget per meal or per week
- Preferred cuisines or flavor profiles

## Process
1. **Define constraints** — list all non-negotiables (allergies, religious restrictions, equipment limits).
2. **Select cooking methods** — batch cooking, one-pot meals, sheet-pan dinners, etc.
3. **Build a protein-vegetable-starch framework** — each meal should have a source of protein, at least one vegetable, and a starch or grain.
4. **Plan for leftovers** — cook once, eat twice (e.g., roast chicken → chicken salad → chicken soup).
5. **Balance effort** — mix quick meals (under 30 min) with weekend projects.
6. **Create a shopping list** — group by grocery store aisle.

## Output
- Weekly meal grid (day × meal)
- Shopping list
- Prep timeline (what to do on Sunday vs. day-of)
