# Dietary Assessment Template

## Purpose
Evaluate the user's current dietary intake against evidence-based guidelines and identify areas for improvement.

## Inputs
- Food diary or recall (typical day)
- Goals and concerns
- Any known deficiencies or lab results (if shared)

## Process
1. **Analyze macro distribution** compared to AMDRs.
2. **Assess micronutrient adequacy** (fiber, calcium, iron, vitamin D, B12, etc.).
3. **Identify patterns** (e.g., low vegetable intake, high added sugar, insufficient protein at breakfast).
4. **Compare to dietary guidelines** (e.g., Dietary Guidelines for Americans, DASH, Mediterranean).
5. **Prioritize changes** that would have the biggest impact on health and goals.

## Output
- Summary of current intake
- Strengths and weaknesses
- Top 3 actionable recommendations
- Optional: sample swaps or additions

## Constraints
- Do not diagnose deficiencies based on diet alone; recommend blood work if suspected.
- Do not shame the user's current diet; focus on positive changes.
- Keep recommendations realistic and sustainable.
