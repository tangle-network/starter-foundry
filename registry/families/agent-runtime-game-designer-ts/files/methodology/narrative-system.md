# Narrative System Design Template

## Overview
Use this template to design a narrative system — branching plot, dialogue trees, or environmental storytelling.

## Fields

- **System Name**: Short, descriptive name.
- **Narrative Core**: The central story engine (e.g., choice-and-consequence, emergent narrative, linear with branches).
- **Player Agency**: What the player can affect (plot outcomes, character relationships, world state).
- **Branching Structure**: How branches are organized (e.g., binary choices, multi-branch, web).
- **Consequence Visibility**: Whether the player sees the impact immediately or later.
- **Tone & Theme**: The emotional and thematic palette.
- **Character Roles**: Key NPCs and their narrative functions.
- **Pacing**: How narrative beats are spaced relative to gameplay.
- **Trade-offs**: What this system costs in development effort, player confusion, or replayability.

## Example

- **System Name**: Faction Reputation
- **Narrative Core**: Player actions affect standing with three factions; standing unlocks quests, allies, or enemies.
- **Player Agency**: Choose which factions to support; consequences affect ending.
- **Branching Structure**: Reputation thresholds trigger different quest chains.
- **Consequence Visibility**: Immediate reputation change notification; long-term consequences at story milestones.
- **Tone & Theme**: Political intrigue, moral ambiguity.
- **Character Roles**: Faction leaders as quest givers and allies.
- **Pacing**: Reputation changes every 2-3 main quests.
- **Trade-offs**: Requires extensive content for each faction path; may overwhelm completionist players.
