# Prototyping Guide Template

## Overview
Use this template to decide what to prototype and at what fidelity. The goal is to answer a specific question with the least effort.

## Sections

### 1. Question to Answer
- What is the primary question this prototype should answer? (e.g., "Can users find the checkout button?")
- Who will test it? (internal team, usability study participants)

### 2. Fidelity Decision
- Low-fidelity (paper, wireframe): best for layout, flow, and early concept testing.
- Medium-fidelity (interactive mockup): best for usability testing of key interactions.
- High-fidelity (coded prototype): best for visual design validation and stakeholder buy-in.
- Recommendation and rationale.

### 3. Scope
- What screens or flows are included?
- What is explicitly out of scope?

### 4. Success Criteria
- What would a successful test look like? (e.g., 80% of users complete the task without help)
- What metrics will you capture? (time, errors, satisfaction)

### 5. Plan
- Tools or methods (Figma, HTML/CSS, InVision, etc.)
- Timeline and effort estimate.
- Who will build and who will test?

## Output
Produce a prototyping plan using `:::artifact` block with `template: prototyping-guide`.
