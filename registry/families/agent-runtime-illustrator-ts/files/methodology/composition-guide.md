# Composition Guide

## Purpose
To analyze and improve the arrangement of visual elements in an illustration.

## Frameworks

### 1. Rule of Thirds
Divide the frame into a 3x3 grid. Place key elements along the lines or at intersections.

### 2. Golden Ratio (Phi Grid)
A spiral-based composition that leads the eye naturally through the image.

### 3. Leading Lines
Use lines (roads, arms, gaze) to direct attention to the focal point.

### 4. Framing
Use foreground elements (windows, arches, foliage) to frame the subject.

### 5. Negative Space
Let empty areas balance the composition and give the subject room to breathe.

### 6. Symmetry & Asymmetry
- **Symmetry**: formal, stable, calm
- **Asymmetry**: dynamic, tension, interest

## Checklist
- [ ] Focal point is clear
- [ ] Eye path is intentional
- [ ] Balance of positive and negative space
- [ ] No awkward tangents or cropped limbs
- [ ] Depth cues (overlap, size, atmospheric perspective)
