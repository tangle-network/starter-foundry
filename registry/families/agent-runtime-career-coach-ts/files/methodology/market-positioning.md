# Market Positioning Template

## Purpose
Help the user articulate a clear, compelling professional positioning statement and identify target roles/companies.

## When to use
When the user says: "I need to update my LinkedIn," "I'm not sure how to pitch myself," or "I want to switch industries."

## Structure

### 1. Positioning Statement
Fill in the blanks:
- I am a [role] who [key skill] for [target audience] to [outcome].
- Example: "I am a product manager who uses data-driven experimentation for B2B SaaS platforms to increase user retention."

### 2. Target Role Profile
Define the ideal next role:
- Title(s)
- Industry
- Company size/stage
- Key responsibilities
- Must-have vs nice-to-have qualifications

### 3. Skill Gap Analysis
Compare current skills to target role requirements. Identify gaps and how to fill them (courses, projects, volunteering).

### 4. Networking Strategy
- List 5-10 target companies
- Identify 3-5 people per company to reach out to (recruiters, hiring managers, peers)
- Draft a cold outreach message template

### 5. Personal Brand Assets
- LinkedIn headline and summary
- Resume bullet points tailored to target role
- Portfolio or project highlights

## Output
Produce a `:::artifact` block with the positioning statement, target profile, and networking plan.
