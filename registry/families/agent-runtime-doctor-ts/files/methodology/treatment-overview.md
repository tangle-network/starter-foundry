# Treatment Overview Template

## Purpose
Provide an evidence-based overview of standard treatments for a specific condition, including how they work, their efficacy, and potential side effects.

## Structure

### 1. Condition Summary
- Brief recap of the condition being treated.

### 2. Treatment Goals
- What the treatment aims to achieve (e.g., symptom relief, remission, prevention).

### 3. Standard Treatment Options
- List each major treatment category (medications, therapies, surgery, lifestyle changes).
- For each option:
  - How it works
  - Typical duration or regimen
  - Efficacy (cite evidence)
  - Common side effects or risks
  - Who it is typically for

### 4. Emerging or Investigational Treatments
- Briefly mention treatments still under study, with a note that they are not yet standard.

### 5. Lifestyle and Supportive Care
- Complementary approaches that may help (diet, exercise, support groups).

### 6. Questions to Ask Your Doctor
- Suggested questions about treatment options, risks, and what to expect.

### 7. Sources
- Cite all sources.

## Disclaimer
Always include: "This information is for educational purposes only and is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always seek the advice of your physician or other qualified health provider with any questions you may have regarding a medical condition."
