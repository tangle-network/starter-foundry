# Opportunity Scoring Template

Use this template to score an opportunity against MEDDIC/MEDDPICC criteria. Each dimension is scored 0–3 (0 = absent, 1 = weak, 2 = moderate, 3 = strong). Total score out of 21 (MEDDIC) or 24 (MEDDPICC).

## Dimensions

### Metrics (M)
- Does the prospect have quantifiable business impact? (revenue, cost, time)
- Have they shared specific numbers?
- Score: ___

### Economic Buyer (E)
- Have you identified the person with budget authority?
- Do you have direct access?
- Score: ___

### Decision Criteria (D)
- Do you know the formal criteria they will use to evaluate?
- Are you shaping those criteria?
- Score: ___

### Decision Process (D)
- Do you know the steps, timeline, and stakeholders?
- Is the process documented?
- Score: ___

### Identify Pain (I)
- Is there a clear, acknowledged pain point?
- Is it urgent and important to the buyer?
- Score: ___

### Champion (C)
- Do you have a verified internal advocate?
- Do they have influence and access to the EB?
- Score: ___

### Competition (C)
- Do you know who else is being evaluated?
- Do you know your competitive position?
- Score: ___

### Paper Process (P) — optional
- Is there a procurement process with required documentation?
- Have you started it?
- Score: ___

### Implication (I) — optional
- Have you quantified the cost of inaction?
- Is the pain tied to a strategic priority?
- Score: ___

## Scoring Guide

- **18+ (MEDDIC) / 21+ (MEDDPICC)**: Strong deal — likely to close.
- **12–17 / 14–20**: Moderate — gaps exist; focus on low scores.
- **Below 12 / 14**: Weak — consider disqualifying or re-qualifying.

## Output

Produce an `:::artifact` block with the scorecard and top 3 gaps to address.
