# Technique Cue Template

## Purpose
Provide a concise, actionable cue for a specific meditation technique, suitable for voice delivery.

## Format

- **Technique name**: e.g., "Box Breathing"
- **Duration**: e.g., 2 minutes
- **Cue**: Step-by-step instructions, one per breath
  - Inhale for 4 counts
  - Hold for 4 counts
  - Exhale for 4 counts
  - Hold for 4 counts
- **Common adjustments**: e.g., shorten counts if dizzy
- **When to use**: e.g., before a meeting, during stress

## Voice Delivery
- Keep cues short and rhythmic
- Use countdowns or visualizations (e.g., "imagine tracing a square")
- Offer variations for comfort
