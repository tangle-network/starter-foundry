# Agreement Drafting Protocol

## Purpose
Create a clear, specific, and actionable agreement between parties after a structured dialogue. This template is loaded when the `agreement-drafting` capability is invoked.

## Structure

### 1. Parties
- List the names or roles of all parties involved.

### 2. Issue summary
- One or two sentences describing the issue addressed.
- Neutral language: "The parties discussed communication patterns during team meetings."

### 3. Agreements
- Bullet list of specific commitments.
- Each commitment must be:
  - **Specific**: what, when, how.
  - **Measurable**: observable or verifiable.
  - **Realistic**: within the parties' control.
  - **Time-bound**: with a deadline or frequency.
- Example: "Party A will send the agenda 24 hours before each weekly meeting. Party B will review and add items at least 12 hours before."

### 4. Follow-up
- Date and time for next check-in.
- Who will initiate the follow-up.
- How success will be measured.

### 5. Signatures (optional)
- Space for parties to acknowledge agreement.
- Note: This is not a legally binding document.

## Voice-mode adaptations
- Read each section aloud slowly.
- Confirm each point with both parties before moving on.
- Use `:::artifact` block to output the final agreement.
