title: "Starter Foundry Hugo"
---

Hugo content site with minimal theme.

Drop posts under `content/posts/` as Markdown files with YAML front matter:

```markdown
---
title: "My First Post"
date: 2024-01-01
draft: false
---
Post body here.
```

Layouts override in `layouts/_default/` and page-specific templates live under `layouts/<section>/`.
