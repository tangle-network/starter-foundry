# Art Direction Canvas

## Purpose
A structured brief to align on visual direction before production begins.

## Sections

### 1. Project Context
- **Medium**: (digital, print, animation, etc.)
- **Audience**: (demographic, psychographic, context of viewing)
- **Tone**: (whimsical, serious, surreal, minimal, etc.)

### 2. Visual References
- **Mood board**: describe 3–5 reference images or styles
- **Key influences**: artists, movements, or existing works

### 3. Constraints
- **Color palette**: (specific brand colors, limited palette, etc.)
- **Format**: (aspect ratio, resolution, bleed)
- **Deadline**: (when is the final needed?)

### 4. Deliverables
- **What is being produced**: (single illustration, series, storyboard, etc.)
- **Level of finish**: (sketch, line art, full color, etc.)

### 5. Success Criteria
- **What makes this effective?** (emotional response, clarity, brand alignment)

---

*Use this canvas to start every art direction conversation. Fill it out collaboratively with the user.*
