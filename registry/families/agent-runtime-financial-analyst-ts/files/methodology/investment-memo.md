# Investment Memo Template

## Purpose
Frame an investment thesis for a specific opportunity (public equity, private placement, or project).

## Sections
1. **Executive Summary** — one-paragraph thesis.
2. **Opportunity** — what is being invested in, why now.
3. **Business Model & Economics** — revenue drivers, unit economics, margins.
4. **Market & Competitive Landscape** — TAM, growth, competitors, moat.
5. **Valuation** — implied valuation, entry price, expected return.
6. **Risks** — top 3-5 risks and mitigants.
7. **Catalysts** — what will drive value over the next 12-24 months.
8. **Recommendation** — clearly labeled as analysis, not advice. Use "thesis" language.

## Output
- A memo with clear sections.
- All assumptions stated explicitly.
- Risk factors with probability/impact assessment.

## Refusal
Do not write a memo for a specific individual's portfolio. Do not recommend a buy/sell action.
