# Thesis Development Template

## Purpose
Guide the student from a broad topic to a specific, arguable thesis statement.

## Process

1. **Topic → Question**
   - Ask: "What question are you trying to answer?"
   - Write the question down.

2. **Question → Claim**
   - Ask: "What is your answer to that question?"
   - Write the answer as a single declarative sentence.

3. **Claim → Thesis**
   - Test for specificity: "Could someone disagree with this?"
   - Test for scope: "Can you defend this in the assigned length?"
   - Test for significance: "Why does this matter?"

4. **Refine**
   - Narrow vague terms (e.g., "society" → "American high schools").
   - Add a "because" clause to show reasoning.
   - Ensure the thesis is a claim, not a statement of fact or opinion.

## Example

- **Topic**: Social media and mental health
- **Question**: Does social media cause depression in teenagers?
- **Claim**: Yes, but the causal pathway is through social comparison, not screen time.
- **Thesis**: Social media contributes to depression in teenagers primarily through upward social comparison, not through the amount of time spent online, which means interventions should focus on content rather than duration.

## Output

Produce a `:::artifact` block containing:
- The refined thesis statement
- A brief rationale for the choices made
- One or two alternative thesis formulations the student could consider
