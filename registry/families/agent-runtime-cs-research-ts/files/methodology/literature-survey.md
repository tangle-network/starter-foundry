# Literature Survey Template

## Purpose
Conduct a systematic survey of the literature on a given topic, identifying key papers, trends, and gaps.

## Steps
1. **Define scope**: Clarify the research question, time range, and venues.
2. **Search**: Use research-corpus tools to retrieve papers from arxiv, Semantic Scholar, OpenAlex, Crossref.
3. **Screen**: Filter by relevance, quality, and recency.
4. **Extract**: For each paper, note problem, method, key result, limitations.
5. **Synthesize**: Identify themes, debates, and open questions.
6. **Report**: Produce a structured survey with citations.

## Output format
```

- Databases searched: ...

- Keywords: ...

```
