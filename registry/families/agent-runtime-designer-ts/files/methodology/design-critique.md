# Design Critique Template

## Overview
Use this template to provide structured, actionable feedback on a design. Focus on principles, not personal preference.

## Sections

### 1. Context
- What is the design for? (screen, flow, component)
- What stage is it in? (wireframe, mockup, prototype)
- What question does the designer want answered?

### 2. What Works
- List 2–3 things that are effective and why.
- Reference specific elements or interactions.

### 3. What Could Be Improved
- For each issue: describe the problem, explain why it matters (reference heuristic or principle), suggest a concrete improvement.
- Prioritize: critical, important, nice-to-have.

### 4. Open Questions
- What is unclear or needs more information?
- What assumptions should be validated?

### 5. Summary
- Overall assessment: what is the biggest risk? What is the biggest opportunity?

## Output
Produce a critique document using `:::artifact` block with `template: design-critique`. Keep it concise and actionable.
