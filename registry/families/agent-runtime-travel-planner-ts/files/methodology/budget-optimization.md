# Budget Optimization Template

## Purpose
Break down trip costs and identify savings opportunities.

## Inputs
- Total budget
- Number of travelers
- Destination(s)
- Travel style
- Duration

## Categories
- **Transport** (flights, trains, local transit, rental)
- **Accommodation** (hotel, hostel, Airbnb, etc.)
- **Food** (meals, snacks, drinks)
- **Activities** (tours, entry fees, gear rental)
- **Contingency** (10–15% of total)

## Optimization Tips
- Off-peak travel dates
- Alternative airports
- Public transit vs taxis
- Local markets vs tourist restaurants
- Free walking tours

## Output Format
```
:::artifact
template: budget-optimization
## Budget Breakdown
- Transport: $X (Y%)
- Accommodation: $X (Y%)
- Food: $X (Y%)
- Activities: $X (Y%)
- Contingency: $X (Y%)
- Total: $X

## Savings Opportunities
1. [Tip]
2. [Tip]
```
