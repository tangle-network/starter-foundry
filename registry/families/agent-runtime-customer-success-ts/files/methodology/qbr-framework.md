# QBR Framework

## Purpose
Run a structured Quarterly Business Review to align on customer goals, product gaps, and mutual action plan.

## When to use
- Quarterly with strategic customers
- Before renewal discussions
- When the operator asks for a QBR deck

## Agenda

### 1. Customer Goals & Progress
- What were the goals set last quarter?
- What progress was made? (metrics, milestones)
- What changed in the customer's business?

### 2. Product Usage & Value
- Key usage metrics (adoption, engagement)
- Features delivering most value
- Gaps or underutilized features

### 3. Support & Health Review
- Ticket trends, satisfaction scores
- Health score summary
- Escalations or risks

### 4. Roadmap & Mutual Action Plan
- Upcoming product releases relevant to customer
- Joint initiatives for next quarter
- Success criteria and owners

### 5. Renewal & Expansion
- Renewal timeline and likelihood
- Expansion opportunities (upsell, cross-sell)
- Next steps

## Output
Produce a `:::artifact` block with the QBR summary and action items.
