# 1:1 Cadence Template

## Purpose
Build trust, surface blockers, align on growth, and detect early
warning signs (burnout, attrition risk, cross-team friction). A 1:1 is
not a status update — managers can read Jira; status meetings are
where status goes. The 1:1 is the conversation that has to happen
between two people for it to matter.

## Frequency

- **Weekly, 30 minutes**, default.
- **Bi-weekly, 45 minutes** for senior ICs who explicitly prefer
  longer-less-frequent (acceptable, but not the default).
- **Skip-level once per quarter** with each team member's manager's
  manager (only for the EM, not for the agent to schedule).
- **Never cancel** the 1:1. Reschedule within 5 business days. A
  cancelled 1:1 with no reschedule is a trust signal — the report
  will read it as "I matter less than this other thing."

## Agenda (suggested 30 min split)

1. **Check-in (2–3 min).**
   - "How are you doing — not work, just generally?"
   - Read energy: tone, posture (in person), pacing of speech (remote).
   - This is not throat-clearing. The signal you'll catch here often
     doesn't surface in the tactical block.
2. **Their agenda (10–12 min).**
   - The report sets the agenda 24h before in a shared doc. If empty,
     the manager prompts: "What's on your mind?" Do *not* fill the
     space with the manager's items first.
   - Common topics: blockers, decisions needed from the manager,
     interpersonal friction, frustration with priorities.
3. **Manager's agenda (5–7 min).**
   - Feedback (specific, recent — never save it for review season).
   - Direction / context the report needs ("here's what leadership
     is thinking about for next quarter").
   - Cross-team coordination asks.
4. **Growth (5–7 min).**
   - "What did you learn this week?" or "What's a skill you want to
     build?"
   - Specific stretch assignments tied to the report's growth area.
   - Career framing once per month minimum: where they want to be
     in 12 months, what evidence the org needs to see.
5. **Close (2 min).**
   - Action items captured (theirs and yours), with owners.
   - Confirm next 1:1.
   - "Anything else you want to put on next week's agenda?"

## Topic rotation

Across a month, every 1:1 should touch:
- Tactical / blockers (every week)
- Career / growth (≥1 of 4)
- Feedback (≥1 of 4 — feedback is not annual)
- Personal check-in beyond work (≥1 of 4 — relationship grounding)
- Direction / strategy (≥1 of 4)

Don't run all 4 weeks tactical-only. The relationship goes shallow,
and the first you'll hear of a real problem is a resignation.

## Listening discipline

- **Talk-time target: 70/30** — report does 70% of the talking.
- **Reflect before responding.** "It sounds like the hardest part is …"
- **Don't fix in the room.** Coach, don't rescue. "What would you
  try first?" before "here's what I'd do."
- **Note the words you didn't say.** If the report mentions a peer
  three times negatively, that's a pattern worth a follow-up — but
  the 1:1 is not the place to litigate it on the spot.

## Signals to escalate (from a 1:1 to a real action)

- Burnout language: "I haven't taken a real weekend in …" / "I'm
  tired all the time" / "I don't know if I can keep doing this."
- Attrition risk: "I've been thinking about my next move" / "I'm
  not sure this is the right place for me" / "I had coffee with a
  friend who's hiring."
- Cross-team conflict: same name comes up negatively across multiple
  reports.
- Compensation concern: market data references, "I haven't had a
  raise in …", peer-comparison language.
- Health / family: should trigger an offer of flexibility (PTO,
  reduced hours, leave) before they ask.

## Notes hygiene

- Shared doc, dated entries. Both parties can edit.
- Action items at the top of each entry, struck through when done.
- Don't put feedback in a private-only doc — that's how it gets
  forgotten before review season. Put it in the shared 1:1 doc.

## Output block

If the agent is asked to draft a 1:1 agenda or summarize a 1:1 round
across the team, wrap in `:::artifact` with
`template: one-on-one-cadence`. Never put real names of reports in
generated artifacts unless the user has provided them and consented
to that scope.

## Refusal

The agent will not:
- Generate a "performance issue" memo from a 1:1 transcript without
  HR involvement.
- Recommend firing or PIP-placing a named report — that's a human +
  HR + skip-level decision.
- Surface a 1:1 transcript to anyone other than the manager and
  report without explicit consent.
