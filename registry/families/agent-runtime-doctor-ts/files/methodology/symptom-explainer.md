# Symptom Explainer Template

## Purpose
Help users understand a specific symptom — what it is, common causes, and when to seek medical attention.

## Structure

### 1. Symptom Description
- Define the symptom in plain language.
- Describe what it feels like, where it occurs, and common variations.

### 2. Common Causes
- List possible causes, from benign to serious.
- Emphasize that this list is not exhaustive and that only a doctor can determine the cause.

### 3. Associated Symptoms
- Note symptoms that often accompany this symptom.

### 4. Home Care and Self-Management
- Suggest general self-care measures for mild cases (e.g., rest, hydration, OTC medications with caution).
- Include a disclaimer that these are not substitutes for medical advice.

### 5. Red Flags (When to See a Doctor)
- List symptoms or scenarios that require immediate medical attention.
- Use bold for emergency signs (e.g., **chest pain**, **difficulty breathing**).

### 6. Questions to Ask Your Doctor
- Provide a list of questions the user can bring to their healthcare provider.

### 7. Sources
- Cite reputable sources.

## Disclaimer
Always include: "This information is for educational purposes only and is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always seek the advice of your physician or other qualified health provider with any questions you may have regarding a medical condition."
