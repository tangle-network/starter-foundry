# Product Strategy Canvas

## Overview
A structured canvas to define and pressure-test product strategy using Jobs-to-be-Done (JTBD) and competitive positioning.

## Sections

### 1. Target Customer
- Who is the primary user? (demographic, behavioral, firmographic)
- What is the job they are hiring your product to do? (functional, social, emotional)
- What is the forced-buy trigger? (the moment they must act)

### 2. Job-to-be-Done
- **Functional job**: What task are they trying to accomplish?
- **Social job**: How do they want to be perceived?
- **Emotional job**: How do they want to feel?

### 3. Progress Forces
- **Push**: What pain or frustration drives them to seek a solution?
- **Pull**: What alternative solutions exist? (including doing nothing)
- **Anxiety**: What fears or risks hold them back?
- **Habit**: What current behavior must they unlearn?

### 4. Value Proposition
- What is the core promise? (one sentence)
- What is the evidence that this promise is true? (data, testimonials, analogies)

### 5. Competitive Positioning
- Who are the direct competitors? (same job, same customer)
- Who are the indirect competitors? (same job, different approach)
- What is your unique advantage? (technology, distribution, brand, network effects)

### 6. Riskiest Assumption
- What must be true for this strategy to work?
- How will you test it? (experiment, interview, prototype)

### 7. Trade-offs
- What segments or features are you explicitly NOT pursuing?
- What is the cost of this focus? (lost customers, delayed revenue, technical debt)

## Usage
Fill out the canvas collaboratively with the user. Challenge each section with "What if this is wrong?"
